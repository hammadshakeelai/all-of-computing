# Starting resources for a CS/AI learning map

Official pages checked 2026-10-06. This is a short selection to choose from after diagnostics, not a list to study in parallel or a substitute for your BSAI syllabus. The choice guidance and suggested evidence tasks are our recommendations; the providers do not endorse this project.

| Need | Official resource | When to consider it | Suggested evidence task |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Broad programming/CS foundations | [Harvard CS50x](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) | If introductory programming, memory, algorithms, and data structures need consolidation | Solve a new small problem and explain correctness and design |
| Algorithms and data structures | [MIT 6.006, Spring 2020](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-spring-2020/) | After checking the course syllabus and your programming/math readiness | Implement an algorithm and justify correctness and complexity |
| Linear algebra | [MIT 18.06, Spring 2010](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/) | When vector spaces, matrix operations, or eigenvalues block AI understanding | Work a small example and explain its geometric meaning |
| Machine learning | [Stanford CS229](https://cs229.stanford.edu/) | When programming, linear algebra, calculus, and probability are ready for the selected material | Derive a simple model and compare it with a reproducible baseline |
| Deep learning | [Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/) | When foundational model training and mathematical notation are comfortable | Reproduce a small experiment and explain its failure cases |
| Wider self-study coverage | [OSSU Computer Science](https://github.com/ossu/computer-science) | To inspect coverage and prerequisites beyond your current modules | Select one gap, rather than adopting the whole sequence immediately |

Historical MIT courses are explicitly dated: their foundational topics can be useful, while software setup and some examples may require adjustment. CS229 and other current pages change across offerings; check the selected syllabus and public material before planning around it. Access to teaching material does not imply enrolled grading, certification, or degree credit.

## Using a resource in the current app

1. Register the resource and its official URL in the course/resource tracker.
2. Add the few concepts needed for your active outcome, using your own notes or licensed material.
3. Create a learning path with an outcome and ordered concepts.
4. Study and answer in the app; use the provider's own environment for external assignments.
5. Link artifact locations in notes. Record hints, tool use, and outside assistance.
6. Track course progress manually, separately from evidence of independent performance.

Follow each course's academic honesty and content-sharing rules. This app does not sync grades, execute code, enroll in courses, or copy their content automatically. Check available access, budget, and prerequisites before committing significant time. [Scope](SCOPE.md) · [Personal worksheet](PERSONAL-SCOPE.md).
